# Password reset

Staff reset their own password at the self-service portal after confirming their identity with MFA.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and cannot reuse any of the last 10 passwords.
If the account is locked after 5 failed attempts, it unlocks automatically after 15 minutes.
The help desk never asks for your current password by email, phone or chat.
If MFA is unavailable, open a ticket with priority "high" and the help desk will verify identity by video call.
